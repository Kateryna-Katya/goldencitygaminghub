Golden City: Merge Kingdom

A Legendary City Is Waiting
Under the Dust

Merge bricks, gems and golden statues on the board, watch buildings rise from the rubble, and bring an ancient golden city back to life — one tile at a time.

How It Works

Three Tiles. One Kingdom.

The rules take about ten seconds to learn. The city takes a little longer — and that's the fun part.

  • Merge

    Drag matching items together on the grid. Three clay bricks become a stone block. Three stone blocks become a marble slab. Keep going and you'll be holding materials fit for a palace.

  • Build

    Deliver your merged resources to construction sites around the map. Each finished building clears away ruins, opens new paths, and adds a little more gold to the skyline.

  • Restore

    Complete a district and watch it come alive — market stalls open, fountains start running, lanterns light up at dusk. Then a new district appears on the horizon, and the board gets a fresh set of puzzles.

No move limits. No forced waiting. If you want to sit and merge for an hour, the game will happily let you.
